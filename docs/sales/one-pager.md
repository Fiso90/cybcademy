# CybCademy
### Human Cyber Risk Management, Built for African Organisations

---

**The problem:** Most security awareness training is a once-a-year video and a quiz nobody remembers. It doesn't measure anything, doesn't adapt, and doesn't give you real evidence for auditors — and global platforms built for European and American budgets don't fit African market conditions, payment methods, or regulatory frameworks.

**What CybCademy does:** Treats every employee as a measurable, improvable risk surface — not a compliance checkbox.

---

## What You Get

**Train** — Course authoring and delivery, server-graded assessments, verifiable certificates. Starter content library included so you're not starting from zero.

**Simulate** — Realistic phishing simulations with AI-generated templates, non-punitive by design: employees who click get immediate, constructive education — not blame. Individual results stay confidential by default; managers see department trends, not who clicked what.

**Measure** — The Human Risk Score: one number, per employee, per department, and across your whole organisation, combining training completion, assessment performance, and phishing resilience. Trended over time, visible on an executive dashboard your board will actually understand.

**Prove** — An Audit Centre that generates complete, tamper-evident compliance evidence packages — training records, policy sign-offs, incident logs — in under 5 minutes, not days of spreadsheet archaeology.

**Comply** — Built with POPIA and NDPA in mind from the ground up, not retrofitted. Configurable compliance framework mapping per organisation.
