# CybCademy — User Manual

For employees using CybCademy for security awareness training.

## Getting Started

Log in with the email and password provided by your organisation. If your role requires multi-factor authentication, you'll be prompted to enter a code from your authenticator app after your password.

## Completing Training

Your assigned courses appear on your home screen with a progress bar and, where applicable, a due date. Click a course to open it — lessons are presented in order, and your progress saves automatically as you go, so you can pick up where you left off on any device.

Some courses end with a short assessment. You'll see your score and whether you passed immediately after submitting. Passing issues a certificate to your record automatically — no extra steps needed.

## Phishing Simulations

From time to time, your organisation runs simulated phishing emails as part of your training — these are safe, controlled exercises, not real threats. If you click a simulated link, you'll be shown a short, friendly explanation of what to look out for next time — this is purely educational and is never used to single you out. Your organisation's leadership sees trends across departments, not "who clicked what."

**If you're ever unsure whether an email is real or a simulation, report it anyway** — see below. There's no wrong answer.

## Reporting Something Suspicious

If you see an email, message, or anything else that seems off, use the **Report Phishing** button available from anywhere in the app. It takes seconds:

1. Choose the category that best fits (suspected phishing, suspicious activity, policy concern, or other).
2. Briefly describe what you saw.
3. Submit.

A Security Officer is notified and will follow up if needed. Reporting is never held against you — even if it turns out to be nothing, reporting things you're unsure about is exactly the behaviour this exists to encourage.

## Policies

Your organisation may ask you to review and acknowledge certain policies. You'll see these listed with a clear "Acknowledge" action — read the document, then confirm you've read and understood it. Once acknowledged, that record is permanent (this is intentional — it's what makes it count as real evidence that you reviewed it).

## Your Data

Your training completion, assessment scores, and policy acknowledgements are visible to your organisation's Administrators and Compliance team, since that's the point of the platform. Individual phishing simulation results are visible only to Security/Compliance roles by default — your direct manager sees department-level trends, not your individual results, unless your organisation has specifically configured otherwise (ask your Administrator if you're unsure which applies to you).

## Getting Help

Check the in-app Knowledge Base for quick answers. For anything else, contact your organisation's Administrator.
